### Daily challenge

- Pick your Link and Samus on the Daily screen before you press Start.
- A boss goal shows the boss's icon and where it waits, for example "Defeat Arrghus, in the Swamp Palace in Zelda".
- The Daily screen shows how many players are in the window right now.
- Each window has its own chat on PC, Android and 3DS. Send a quick line (GL HF!, GG, o7...) and see a note
  when someone finishes, with their time and place. The chat closes with the window.
