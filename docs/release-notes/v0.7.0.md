### Online races

You can now race other players on the same seed, starting at the same moment, on PC, Linux handhelds, Android and
3DS. Players on different devices can race each other, as long as everyone has v0.7.0.

- The app opens on a short logo and then a menu: Start / Load game (the run list you already know),
  Online draft, Championship and Options. Without a connection the two online entries show as Offline.
- Race 1 vs 1 or in a multi race of up to 8. Create a private room and share its code, join one with a
  code, or pick a public room from the list. Everyone chooses a Link and a Samus, then confirms in the ready room. The
  race starts when the last player is ready, with a 3, 2, 1 that is the same on every screen.
- Championship is a normal run from the start, as a Full game, a Sprint (2 crystals and 1 Super Metroid boss) or a
  Half (4 crystals and 2 bosses). Save states, fast forward and rewind are off. The winner can put their time on the
  ladder, one table per length.
- Online draft is a short race of 5 to 15 minutes. Everyone starts in the same place, somewhere in either game, with
  the same starting items. The goal shows at the 3, 2, 1: defeat a number of enemies, collect rupees, beat a boss or
  get a crystal. A Draft lasts 30 minutes at most. If nobody reaches the goal, a 1 vs 1 is a draw and a multi race goes
  to the highest %. Drafts have no ladder.
- During a race, a note in the corner tells you when a rival gets a major item, a boss or a crystal. With a
  second screen you also get the standings and a chat tab; on one screen, chat opens from the tracker. In a multi
  race, places show up as players finish, then the final table.
- A Championship Full game can be paused if every player votes yes, and resumed another day at the time the
  vote set. Times from a paused race go on the ladder with a mark.
- If the server goes away, the game goes on and your finish is sent when it is back. If you lose
  your connection, you can come back to the same race within 5 minutes.

### PC dual screen

- The second screen now works like the Thor's tracker: RUN with the map and shortcuts, ITEMS, CHECKS by area, MAP with
  gestures and dungeon panels, HELP and CONFIG.
- Hotkeys as on the Thor, set from CONFIG, a portal between the two games, and a way to leave the run by pad or touch.
- The second screen can be touch only, and shows the Thor's idle screen between runs.
- On handhelds the button legends use the letters printed on the device.
- On every version the menu fades to black and the game starts two seconds after you press the button.
