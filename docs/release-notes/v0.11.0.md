### 3DS

- The online parts (daily challenge, world goals, ladders) now really work on a console. The 3DS could never open a
  secure connection to the server, so it talks to it over plain HTTP.
- The HOME menu calls the app SMZ3 Universe.
- The banner sound on the HOME menu is the first 3 seconds of the SMZ3 intro. Before, it played as a shrill whine.
- Runs are drawn pixel perfect: each SNES pixel is one screen pixel, with no doubled rows or columns and no scanlines.
- New Screen option in the pause menu: Pixel perfect, 4:3, Full height 8:7, Full height 4:3 and Fullscreen.
- New Colours option in the pause menu: Original, Vivid and Intense. The 3DS top screen is pale, so Vivid is the
  default.
- Picking Screen or Colours shows the game behind the menu as it will look. Left and Right change it, A keeps it and B
  goes back to what you had.
- Back to the runs asks first.
- No more short stutter every 10 seconds: the run's progress is written to the SD card in the background.
- Music notes no longer drop out when sound effects play, for example in Super Metroid. The sound chip's changes used
  to be applied in batches every 8 ms, and a short note could start and stop inside one batch without ever sounding.
