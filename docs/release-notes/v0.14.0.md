### World goals

- Shared goals draw an icon of the game being played: Ganon for finishing ALttP runs, a missile for collecting Super
  Metroid items, a heart container or an energy tank for the daily challenges. ALttP and Super Metroid showed SMZ3's
  icons before.

### Tracker

- "Nearly done" lists a region only once half of its checks are done. The first chest of a run no longer puts its
  whole region there.

### PC

- Windows: the weekly seed is matched to the current week by the app itself. Windows' own date formatting does not
  know ISO weeks.
