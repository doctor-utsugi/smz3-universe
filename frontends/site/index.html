Games

SMZ3

A Link to the Past and Super Metroid in one randomized world. Doors between the two games carry you across. The generator is a C port of SMZ3Randomizer, checked seed by seed against the original.

Cozy, Original and Custom runs · monthly seed, weekly run, daily challenge · world goals

A Link to the Past

The Zelda randomizer on its own, ported from the generator behind alttpr.com. Seeds are made on the device from the Japan 1.0 ROM.

Original (alttpr's default) · race presets Casual Boots, Standard, AD Keys · monthly seed, weekly Fast Ganon, daily challenge

Super Metroid

The Super Metroid randomizer on its own, Map Rando included: it rebuilds the whole map, as SM races play it.

Original (Tournament logic, Split placement) · Map Rando Season 5, Map Rando Beginner · monthly seed, weekly run, daily challenge
